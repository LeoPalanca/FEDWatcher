05929dd | 2026-05-13 | mottinellilorenzo | Add StrategistAgent with EWMA tone smoothing
    Introduce agents/strategist.py implementing the time-aware exponentially
    weighted moving average from README.md:
        S_t = alpha_t * tone_score_t + (1 - alpha_t) * S_{t-1}
        alpha_t = 1 - exp(-lambda * Delta t),  lambda = ln(2) / h,  h = 21
    
    Delta t is the calendar-day gap between releases so irregular FOMC
    cadence is handled per AGENTS.md guardrails. The first observation
    seeds the series with alpha = 1.0 to avoid dragging the smoother toward
    zero for months. Returns a SmoothedTone dataclass per observation
    (date, raw tone, smoothed tone, alpha, days_since_prev) for downstream
    use by the signals table and dashboard.
    
    Scope is intentionally limited to smoothing; multinomial nowcast,
    tone-implied rate, and market divergence are deferred until macro and
    market features are wired through pipeline.py.
