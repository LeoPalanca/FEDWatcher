A calm interface for reading the Fed.

§ 00 · PRINCIPLES

Four guardrails for every decision.

Before tokens and components, the system commits to four positions. When in doubt, return here.

01

Numbers are the subject.

Tabular monospace numerals, generous whitespace, restrained chrome. Charts use thin lines and minimal grids — the data carries the weight, not the container.

02

One accent, used sparingly.

A single institutional teal-blue marks intent, focus, and primary action. It never decorates. If everything is highlighted, nothing is.

03

Serif headlines, sans machinery.

A serif voice gives the agent's prose the authority of a research paper; sans-serif and mono carry the interface and the data without competing.

04

Cite or stay silent.

Every claim the agent makes points back to a source — FOMC document, BLS series, speech, dataset. Trust is built from traceability, not confidence.

§ 01 · TYPOGRAPHY

Source Serif 4. Inter. JetBrains Mono.

A scholarly serif for headings and the agent's voice; a neutral sans for the interface; a monospace for everything numeric, tabular, or technical. Three families, total.

§ 06 · AGENT & CITATIONS

The agent reads like a research note.

FEDWatcher's prose lives in a roomy column with serif headlines and inline citations. No avatars, no chrome, no chat bubbles — every claim is anchored to a source, and sources are listed at the bottom of each turn.

You · 14:08

Compare the dispersion of participant projections between the December 2025 and March 2026 SEPs. Was the consensus tighter?

FEDWatcher · 14:08

Yes — measurably. Across the three core variables, participant dispersion in the March 2026 SEP narrowed relative to December 2025, with the largest tightening on the fed funds path.

For the 2026 fed funds midpoint, the high-low range fell from 1.50 percentage points in December to 1.00 in March[1]. Core PCE dispersion narrowed from 0.7 to 0.5 points[1], and the unemployment range was unchanged at 0.5 points.

Reading this alongside the statement, the implication is that participants are converging on a slower easing path. Powell's press conference framed this as a function of stickier services inflation rather than a re-evaluation of the long-run neutral rate[2].

§ 07 · ICONOGRAPHY

Thin strokes, square corners, no novelty.

Icons are 16-grid, 1.5px stroke, monoline. Reach for Lucide or Phosphor (regular weight). Avoid filled glyphs except for currently-active states; never use multi-color or skeumorphic icons.

§ 08 · WORDMARK

Two voices set in type.

FEDWatcher is set in Source Serif 4. The first half is upright Regular — the institution. The second half is italic Light, in accent — the agent watching it. No logomark; the contrast is the mark.

DO

Set "FED" upright, regular weight; set "Watcher" italic, light, in accent. Optionally close with a period in accent.

DO NOT

Apply gradients, drop-shadows, or glyph icons. Do not space the two halves apart, hyphenate, or change the case.

CLEAR SPACE

Keep at least the cap height of "F" as breathing room on every side. Minimum legible size: 14px on screen, 9pt in print.
