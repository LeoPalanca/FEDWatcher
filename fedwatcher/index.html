Why these weights?

The analyst agent splits each release into sections, scores each section's tone independently, then weights them by how predictive they have historically been of the next FOMC decision.

Forward-guidance sentences move the most; the inflation paragraph is second. The general-context block carries almost no signal on its own — it is included for completeness.

Distribution · read Right-skewed

Mass concentrated on a hold, with a hawkish skew.

The modal outcome is a hold at 55%, but the distribution leans right: cumulative hike probability (+25 and +50 bps) sits at 27% versus a cumulative cut probability of 18%. The May statement's new “inflation has been somewhat elevated” line is the largest single contributor to that skew.

Two consecutive softer CPI prints would flip the distribution back to symmetric within one meeting cycle; a third hawkish surprise would compress mass at +25 bps and make a hike the modal outcome.

FedWatcher's read of the Committee's language (in blue) versus what the OIS market is pricing (in gray, dashed). Divergence is reported as market − tone (in pp): the shaded gap is green when tone runs above the market (market underestimates, tone is more hawkish; divergence < 0) and red when tone runs below (market overestimates, tone is more dovish; divergence > 0). Historically, market expectations have moved toward the tone-implied path within two meetings.
