Project Brief

FedWatcher monitors Federal Reserve communication, extracts policy tone with an LLM-backed analyst agent, joins macro data from FRED, and presents rate-move probabilities through a FastAPI-backed dashboard.

This static page is the brief homepage/dashboard placeholder for `fedwatcher.ellep.it` while the API and Streamlit/FastAPI app are implemented.
