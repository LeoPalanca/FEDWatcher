§ 05 · BRIEF

Four guardrails for every decision.

FedWatcher is a course prototype. The principles below describe how the dashboard should behave when more data, better models, or richer narratives arrive.

01

Numbers are the subject.

Tabular monospace numerals, generous whitespace, restrained chrome. Charts use thin lines and minimal grids — the data carries the weight.

02

One accent, used sparingly.

A single institutional teal-blue marks intent and primary action. It never decorates. If everything is highlighted, nothing is.

03

Serif headlines, sans machinery.

A serif voice gives the agent's prose the authority of a research paper; sans and mono carry the interface and the data without competing.

04

Cite or stay silent.

Every claim points back to a source — FOMC document, BLS series, speech, dataset. Trust is built from traceability, not confidence.
